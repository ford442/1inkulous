and the trees are geometry generated by code (`src/engine/mesh/`), and every

## Adding an asset

An asset may be added only if it is original, or its licence lets a game
redistribute it (CC0, CC-BY, MIT and the like). Not allowed: non-commercial or
no-derivatives licences, "free for personal use", and anything ripped from
another game — including Populous, whose art, sound and names belong to their
owners.

Add the row to the table above in the same change as the file, with where it
came from, who made it and under what licence, and keep any attribution the
licence asks for. `npm run test:assets` fails if a file in `public/models` or
`public/audio` is not named in this file.
